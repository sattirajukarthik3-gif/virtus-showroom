05 · Chassis

MQB A0 IN

Body rendered transparent. McPherson struts up front, a twist-beam axle at the rear; springs compress and rebound as the platform absorbs the road.

Ventilated disc brakes

Built to shed heat

Front discs are ventilated to dissipate heat during repeated braking; the rear axle uses drum brakes. Electric power steering acts through a rack-and-pinion.

06 · Wheels & tyres

205/55 R16

Sixteen-inch alloys on the upper variants (195/65 R15 on Comfortline). Disc, caliper and hub sit inside the rim.

Contact patch

Rolling road

Everything the car does — acceleration, braking, steering — passes through four patches of rubber about the size of your palm.

07 · Exterior design

LED headlights

Designed to create a distinctive night-time signature; LED daytime running lights frame the cluster.

Grille

Chrome-lined, low and wide

Horizontal slats stretch the front visually; the VW roundel sits on the centre line.

Hood

Long, creased bonnet

Two power lines run from the grille toward the A-pillars — the sedan's long-hood proportion starts here.

Body

Character line

A single crease runs the length of the flanks, tightening the surface between the wheel arches.

Doors

Four doors, one shoulder

Door skins carry the line unbroken; body-coloured handles keep the surface calm.

Wheels

Alloy wheels

Diamond-cut spokes on the upper trims fill the arches of a 2,651 mm wheelbase.

Rear

LED tail lamps

A wide boot lid over a 521-litre boot; chrome strip and lamps stretch the rear.

08 · Interior

Enter the cabin

Through the driver's door. Hotspots mark every touch point — the cockpit, the screen, the seats.

Digital cockpit

20.32 cm instrument display

Speed, revs, gear and range on one configurable screen. Available on the higher variants.

Infotainment

25.4 cm touchscreen

Navigation, media, phone, vehicle data and climate — with wireless App-Connect for Apple CarPlay and Android Auto.

Steering wheel

Exploded view

Airbag module, leather rim with multifunction controls, DSG paddle shifters and the column — separated so you can see what your hands hold.

Ergonomics

Seats

Driver positioning, thigh and lumbar support, and on the upper trims ventilated front seats. Rear passengers get a 2,651 mm wheelbase's worth of legroom and a folding centre armrest.

09 · Safety

Six airbags

Front, front-side and curtain airbags shown deploying in place. ABS with EBD, electronic stability control and multi-collision brake are standard.

Structure

Safety cell

A rigid passenger cell with defined front and rear deformation zones. Global NCAP: five stars, adult and child occupant protection (2022).
